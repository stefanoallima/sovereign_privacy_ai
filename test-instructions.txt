Navigate to http://localhost:5173 and perform persona integration tests:

STEP 1: App Load
- Go to http://localhost:5173/
- Wait 3 seconds
- Take screenshot of initial state

STEP 2: Console Check
- Open DevTools (F12)
- Go to Console tab
- Take screenshot of console
- Document any errors or warnings

STEP 3: Find Persona Selector
- Look for dropdown or select element with persona options
- Usually in top navigation bar
- Click to open dropdown

STEP 4: Capture Personas
- Take screenshot showing all personas
- Count total personas
- Document each persona name
- Look for emoji icons (brain, target, briefcase, etc.)
- Look for privacy badges (lock, shield, warning)
- Check for group headers

STEP 5: Create Report
- List all personas found
- Show complete console screenshot
- Document groups and icons
- List any errors found
